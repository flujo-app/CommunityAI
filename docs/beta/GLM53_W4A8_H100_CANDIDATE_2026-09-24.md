# GLM-5.3 W4A8 eight-H100 launch candidate

Status: **unqualified candidate**. No backend, model, weight download or GPU run was started. `zai-org/GLM-5.3` official FP8 remains unavailable in the product catalog. `gpustack/GLM-5.3-W4A8` is a separately pinned third-party quantization of that base model, not a replacement for official FP8 qualification.

The pinned [vLLM GLM-5.3 recipe](https://github.com/vllm-project/recipes/blob/127f287593a04d23f9600603785f4cc5530112db/models/zai-org/GLM-5.3.yaml) lists W4A8 as a Hopper variant supporting H100 and H200, with a 447 GB minimum VRAM estimate, expert parallelism, `fp8_ds_mla` KV cache and `--trust-remote-code`. The pinned [GPUStack model card](https://huggingface.co/gpustack/GLM-5.3-W4A8/blob/f6d1e50d43edb5fb3f3141f19fc691511a50756c/README.md) reports actual vLLM load, accuracy and throughput measurements on eight H20-3e devices. Those results do not establish H100 fit, speed or quality for CommunityAI. The card says vLLM does not use the repository's `sitecustomize.py` shim; it is for SGLang. We inspected the small pinned shim and config, but that is not an approved runtime-code review. The repository revision, weight-file bytes and base-model distinction are recorded in `src/drift/model_capacity.py`.

`scripts/prototype_glm53_w4a8_h100_launch.py` first checked the recipe geometry as a standalone pure-Python experiment. `src/drift/managed_vllm_glm53_w4a8_h100.py` then added a guarded argv builder. It requires eight distinct caller-reported H100 devices, at least one eighth of the recipe's 447 GB estimate free on each and at least 447 GB free in aggregate, an existing absolute model directory, a loopback endpoint and the current 2,048-token product context cap. It emits TP8, expert parallelism, bounded single-sequence settings, offline flags and disabled request/usage telemetry. The API key is an environment override, never an argv argument. The returned record explicitly says qualification, remote-code review, quality equivalence and rights approval are false. It is not a process launcher.

The already requested read-only `scripts/probe_volunteer_linux_host.py` now includes a `glm53_w4a8_h100` block using the same one-time GPU/storage inventory. The new block reports prerequisites for the pinned 399,716,726,536-byte artifact and recipe memory estimate, while leaving runtime fit and qualification false. It adds no host mutation, download or GPU workload.

The standalone prototype, focused candidate check and volunteer diagnostic self-test passed locally, each in under a second. The focused check also confirms that the official GLM profile remains unavailable through the generic managed launch gate. No broad CI was run.

Before any trial: obtain the host's actual read-only report and explicit usable access, verify the container/runtime revision and complete pinned code/weight integrity, review `--trust-remote-code` and derivative rights, then perform a bounded supervised H100 load and reference/latency/cancellation test. Do not copy H20 speed or accuracy numbers into the H100 product profile. Full exact-model release still needs independent official FP8 or separately accepted quantized-profile quality evidence on the actual product path.
